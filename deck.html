The problem

Governance is fragmented, action tracking is manual, and executives are drowning in updates.

What happens today

  • SteerCo, MANCO, ARB, TDA, Cyber, Risk, Service — 5+ forums, each in a different tool
  • Actions live in Word minutes, Excel logs, OneNote, personal notebooks
  • Cross-forum patterns invisible until quarterly deep-dives
  • Weekly exec pack takes 3–5 FTE-days to prepare
  • 30–45% of actions lost or aged > 30 days

What it costs

The vision

Three AI capabilities.

1

Source

AI reads every source — meetings, emails, Teams chats, SharePoint documents — extracts actions, and logs them into Jira automatically.

2

Report

AI reports on tickets — updates, status, dashboards, reminders, escalations — administered end-to-end by specialist agents.

3

Analyse

AI analyses documentation to produce cross-source insights for management — themes, risks, decisions, and executive narratives.

Jira is the system of record. AI-administered end-to-end. Humans decide, agents prepare.
