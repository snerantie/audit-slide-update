Architecture Review Board (ARB) — Minutes

Date: Thursday, 9 July 2026, 10:00-12:00 BST
Chair: Elena Rivera (Chief Architect)
Meeting type: Governance (bi-weekly)

Attendees:
- Elena Rivera (Chair, Chief Architect)
- Kenji Tanaka (Cloud & Platform Architect)
- Grace Okafor (Data Architect)
- Robert Alvarez (Payments Architect)
- Hannah Chen (Security Architect)
- Daniel Hughes (Head of Engineering)
- Michael Grant (Enterprise Architect, secretariat)
- Sarah Whitmore (CISO, guest for item 6)
- Priya Deshmukh (Third-Party Risk, guest for item 6)

Agenda:
1. DORA event-logging exception (Payments streaming)
2. Cloud Landing Zone v2 target-state approval
3. Oracle on-prem sunset roadmap
4. Payments event-streaming architecture
5. MFA legacy options (referral from Cyber MANCO)
6. DORA CIF mapping ownership

1 - DORA Event-Logging Exception

Elena Rivera presented the exception request for the Payments streaming platform which emits 47k events per second, exceeding the centralised logging aggregation capacity in the DORA event-logging standard. Decision: exception granted with 30 September 2026 expiry, conditional on a target-state design being presented at the September ARB. New risk R-NEW-2 added to the register at Medium (Payments event-streaming vendor lock-in).

2 - Cloud Landing Zone v2

Kenji Tanaka presented the target-state Cloud Landing Zone v2 blueprint. Highlights: multi-account expansion, PrivateLink-first pattern, workload isolation for regulated data. Decision: approved for detailed design. Kenji Tanaka to publish the LZ v2 blueprint to Confluence by 20 July.

3 - Oracle On-Prem Sunset

Grace Okafor presented the phased Oracle on-prem sunset plan. Two applications remain hard-blocked (TreasuryWorks, LegacyCRM) awaiting replacement decisions. Decision: proceed with phase 1 (16 non-critical databases). Grace Okafor to confirm the sequence of decommissioning and publish a full dependency map by 8 August.

4 - Payments Event-Streaming Architecture

Robert Alvarez presented the options paper for Payments streaming architecture. Three options tabled: (A) migrate to managed Kafka on cloud, (B) event-mesh SaaS, (C) hybrid. Board wanted more depth on option (C). Robert Alvarez to prepare the exception paper and target-state design for review at 30 July ARB.

5 - MFA Legacy Options (referred from Cyber MANCO)

Sarah Whitmore (guest) noted the pending MFA-to-legacy options paper from Cyber MANCO 8 July. ARB to receive Anita Rees's options paper by 25 July for review at 30 July session.

6 - DORA CIF Mapping Ownership

Sarah Whitmore raised the DORA Article 30 CIF (Critical or Important Functions) mapping — surfaced at Cyber MANCO the previous day as an ownership gap. Priya Deshmukh confirmed she will draft the mapping proposal. Decision: Architecture accepts ownership of the CIF mapping, subject to Priya's draft. Elena Rivera to formally close the loop on CIF-mapping ownership with Chief Architect sign-off by 25 July.

Meeting closed at 11:52.
